About Us

Mondo Imports

Mondo Imports was founded in Melbourne over 30 years ago to connect fine continental products with people who love authentic Italian wine. Since then we have grown into a large operation serving customers across the country.

We ship all our products in a controlled environment and bring them to you directly from trusted suppliers. Our long-term, direct relationships help us serve every client with care.
